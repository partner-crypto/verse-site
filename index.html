Day one

Halves first.
Then the whole.

A verse is easier to hold in two pieces. Verse splits each one at its natural pause, has you read both halves twice, then asks you to recite the whole thing without looking.

  • Day one is the only session where you read it
  • Everything after this is recall
The review ladder

Eight reviews.
Then it's yours.

Forgetting moves fastest in the first 24 hours, so the second look comes early. After that the gap keeps growing, until a verse holds for months.

LearningGrowingSettledAfter the final check, a settled verse comes back once a month.
Ten minutes a day

A short queue,
well timed.

Each morning you get only the verses that are due. Nothing piles up, and nothing slips.

New verses arrive at the pace you choose: one, two or four a month. The reviews are spaced for you, so a busy week never buries you.

the schedule.

About us

Made for the verses
you meant to keep.

Verse is a small, independent app for memorizing scripture: a short review each day, timed so what you learn stays.

Why Verse exists

Reading a verse
isn't keeping it.

Most of us have tried to memorize scripture. We read a verse, read it again, and a month later it's gone. Rereading feels like learning, but it isn't recall.

Two things make a verse stick: saying it from memory, and doing that again just before you would forget. Verse is built around both. You learn a verse in halves on day one, and after that every session is recall, spaced so the gaps grow as the verse settles.

The app is named for Psalm 119:11, the first verse you learn in it. That verse is the whole idea: the word, laid up in the heart, where it's there when you need it.

The goal

The app works
when you don't need it.

A verse you've memorized goes where you go: into a hospital waiting room, a hard conversation, a sleepless night. No screen required.

So Verse is built to get out of the way. Ten minutes a day, only the verses that are due, and a schedule that stretches the gaps until a verse holds for months. When a verse settles, the app stops asking for it except for a monthly check.

Privacy & security

Your verses
stay yours.

Verse collects what it needs to schedule your reviews, and nothing it would need to sell.

Passwords are never stored

We keep only a salted, one-way hash of your password. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we keep only the identifier they provide.

Your voice stays on your phone

Recitals are processed on your device by its own speech recognition. We receive the match score, never the recording.

We never see your card

In the app, the App Store confirms your subscription with an anonymized receipt. On the web, payment happens on a secure checkout page.

Encrypted on the way and at rest

Every connection uses TLS, and stored data is encrypted. Access is limited to the few people who need it.

No ads, no data sales

We don't sell personal data or share it for advertising. We honor Global Privacy Control signals.

Your data, your call

Export every verse and its schedule, or delete your account. Deletion finishes within 30 days.
